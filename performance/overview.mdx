Impressions and likes tell you people are paying attention. Growth Intelligence answers the harder questions: are you reaching potential customers, what are you becoming known for, and who's worth following up with.

It connects content performance, brand insights, and lead discovery in one place. Your AI agents read the same signals to decide what to create next.

## On every tab

- **Sync status**, shows when your data last synced and when the next sync runs
- **Explain**, ask Pressmaster to walk you through what a chart or score means
- **Platform filter**, view a single connected channel or all platforms combined
- **Date range**, change the reporting window for every chart on the tab
