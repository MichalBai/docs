Connecting your social media accounts to Pressmaster lets you publish and schedule content directly from the platform, no copy-pasting, no manual posting. Pressmaster supports LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook, and YouTube.

## Supported platforms

- LinkedIn
- Twitter/X
- Instagram
- Threads
- Bluesky
- Facebook
- YouTube

## Connect an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in your Pressmaster dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Click **Integrations** to see all available platforms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a platform">
    Click on the platform you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Pressmaster">
    You'll be redirected to that platform's authorization page. Log in and grant Pressmaster access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Pressmaster">
    After authorizing, you'll be redirected back to Pressmaster. Your account is now connected and ready to publish to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting Bluesky

Bluesky requires one extra step before the redirect. When you click the Bluesky option, enter your Bluesky handle (nickname) first, then complete the authorization on the Bluesky page.

## Connect multiple profiles per platform

You can connect more than one profile on the same platform in a single workspace, for example, your personal LinkedIn profile alongside your company page, or a separate profile for a co-founder or teammate. This replaces the old requirement to use a separate workspace for each additional profile.

Once you've connected additional profiles:

- Pick which profile you're posting to directly in the editor
- Switch between profiles with one click while writing
- Performance analytics are tracked separately for each connected profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Account Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a profile">
    Click **New Profile** and connect the additional account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Profile limits by plan

Multi-profile publishing is included on yearly plans. Monthly plans (other than Partner and Agency Pro) stay at one profile per platform.

| Plan | Profiles per platform |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 |
| Free trial (Leader) | 1 profile on a monthly trial, 2 on a yearly trial |
| Starter (yearly) | 2 |
| Leader (yearly) | 4 |
| Partner | 5 (monthly and yearly) |
| Agency Pro | 5 (monthly and yearly) |

<Note>
  If you're on a monthly plan and want multi-profile publishing, upgrading to yearly billing unlocks it. Check your dashboard for any current upgrade offers.
</Note>

## Connected channels by plan

| Plan | Connected channels |
|---|---|
| Starter | 2 channels |
| Leader | Unlimited |

<Note>
  Pressmaster supports LinkedIn posts but not LinkedIn articles, LinkedIn's API does not allow publishing articles. You can still publish standard LinkedIn posts.
</Note>

## LinkedIn personal vs. business page

To publish to both your personal LinkedIn profile and your company page, connect both as separate profiles using [multiple profiles per platform](#connect-multiple-profiles-per-platform), then pick the one you're posting to from the editor.

<Tip>
  Add links in comments rather than in the post body. Links in posts often reduce reach due to platform algorithms.
</Tip>
