Pressmaster can connect directly to the AI tools you already use through MCP (Model Context Protocol). Once connected, your AI assistant can pull from and act on your Pressmaster workspace without you switching tabs.

**MCP URL:** `https://app.pressmaster.ai/mcp`

MCP is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect directly to external tools. Instead of your assistant telling you what to do in Pressmaster, it does it: creates the draft, attaches the channels, sets the schedule, reads the numbers back.

You don't need an API key. Authentication runs through OAuth in your browser.

## What you can do once connected

- **Content Library**, read and create content
- **Brand Guide and Brand Voice**, reference your brand settings
- **Twin**, retrieve and train on your Twin, plus one-shot chat with it
- **Performance analytics**, pull your content performance data

<Note>
  MCP exposes a curated set of Pressmaster capabilities. It does not give external tools access to Agent Studio chat, your agent's internal prompts, memory, skills, or scheduled tasks.
</Note>

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open MCP settings">
    Go to **Settings > MCP** in your Pressmaster account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your tool">
    Select the tab for your tool: **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the setup guide">
    Follow the 3-step setup guide shown for your tool, using the copyable MCP server URL provided.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start setup from the **Connect** banner on your dashboard, or from the **MCP** card on the **Get Started** page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude">
    Before you start, you'll need an active Pressmaster account and workspace, and Claude on a plan that supports custom connectors (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise).

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open connector settings">
        In Claude (web or desktop), go to **Settings > Connectors**, scroll past the directory, and click **Add custom connector**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the server">
        Paste the URL `https://app.pressmaster.ai/mcp`, name it "Pressmaster", and confirm.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize">
        Claude opens a Pressmaster login window. Sign in, choose which workspace the connection should work in, and approve access.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify">
        Start a new conversation and ask "What's in my Pressmaster content library?" If Claude returns your actual drafts and stages, you're connected.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      You choose which workspace a connection works in when you authorize it. If you belong to more than one workspace, double-check you've selected the right one, you can reconnect to switch later.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ChatGPT">
    Before you start, you'll need an active Pressmaster account and workspace, and a ChatGPT plan that supports custom connectors.

    From the **ChatGPT** tab in **Settings > MCP**, follow the setup guide shown: add a custom connector using the Pressmaster MCP URL, then complete the OAuth sign-in to choose your workspace and authorize it.

    ### The Pressmaster app inside ChatGPT

    Once connected, Pressmaster also appears as an app inside ChatGPT with its own views, not just tool calls:

    - **Content Library**, browse your content as a board or a list
    - **Editing per channel**, open a piece of content and edit the version for a specific channel
    - **Performance**, a view with **Narrative** and **Leads** tabs, matching what you'd see in Pressmaster
    - **Open in Pressmaster**, jump from any item straight to that item in the web app
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Other">
    Any MCP-compatible AI tool can connect the same way. From the **Other** tab in **Settings > MCP**, follow the setup guide shown for adding a custom MCP connector using the Pressmaster MCP URL, then complete the OAuth sign-in to choose your workspace and authorize it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Managing your connection

Go to **Settings > MCP** to see your connection history, every authorization, which tool it was for, and when. The list is scrollable and can be cleared.

## What you can ask

**Create content in any format.** Articles, social posts, newsletters, video scripts. Ask, review in chat, and it lands in your Content Library as a draft.

*"Write a LinkedIn post about why founders confuse their champion with their budget holder. Use my brand voice."*

**Run content interviews.** Start a Pressmaster interview from your AI assistant, answer the questions, and get structured source material back.

*"Start a content interview on go-to-market positioning failures."*

**Write against your Brand Guide.** Your assistant can read your brand guide and voices, so output sounds like you rather than like a model.

**Schedule and publish.** Attach connected channels, set a publish time, push live now, or unschedule a post you've changed your mind about. You can also set a first comment and upload and publish video, including its YouTube category and visibility.

*"Schedule that post to LinkedIn for Thursday at 9am."*

**Read performance data.** Reach, impressions, engagement, follower deltas, and top posts for any date range.

*"Which posts performed best in the last 30 days, and what do they have in common?"*

**Save and load ideas.** Anything worth keeping goes into Saved Ideas and comes back when you need it.

**Train and query your Digital Twin.** Ingest text, run knowledge interviews, and chat with it in the same session.

<Note>
  Actions that would overwrite or publish something ask for confirmation first. Lists come back newest first, with other sort options available, and content totals match what you'd see on the board in Pressmaster.
</Note>

## Who can connect

Besides Claude, ChatGPT, and other named AI tools, MCP supports self-registering custom clients, so any compatible client can register itself without Pressmaster adding it by name first. Agency workspaces can connect the same way as any other workspace.

Access tokens last one hour. Your AI tool refreshes the connection automatically, you shouldn't need to reauthorize unless the refresh itself fails.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Tools don't appear after connecting | Start a new conversation, your AI tool loads the tool list at session start |
| Authorization keeps failing | Confirm you're logged into the correct Pressmaster account in the same browser, clear the session, and retry |
| Analytics return zeros | Expected if nothing is published yet, or a platform sync hasn't run. Ask your assistant to check sync status for per-channel timestamps |
| A post won't schedule | Scheduling needs a connected, credentialed channel and a future timestamp. Reconnect expired channels in Pressmaster first |
| A draft won't update | Only drafts in an editable stage can be changed. Scheduled, published, and completed items are locked, move it back to a draft stage first |
| Your assistant asks for confirmation on every action | Normal. Write and publish actions require your approval before they run |

## Where it's available

MCP is a paid-plan feature, available on the Leader plan and up.

<Tip>
  Content tools normally live in an app you have to remember to open. As an MCP connector, Pressmaster lives inside the conversation where you're already thinking, so an idea can become a scheduled post without a context switch.
</Tip>
