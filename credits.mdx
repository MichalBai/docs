Credits represent AI work done on your behalf. Every time Pressmaster writes, edits, repurposes, plans, analyzes trends, or runs agent reasoning, it draws from your credit balance. You never deal with tokens, compute costs, or technical limits, credits abstract all of that into one simple, predictable quota. A credit isn't a currency: it's a monthly limit within which you can use the platform, and that limit resets to a set level each month.

## What credits cover

Credits are used for any action that involves AI:

- Writing, editing, and repurposing content
- Content planning and ideation
- Trend analysis and hook simulations
- Agent-based reasoning and coordination (Agent Team)

Non-AI features, like browsing the dashboard, managing integrations, or scheduling posts, are always unlimited and never consume credits.

## Credit consumption by action

Credit usage is approximate and may vary slightly based on content length and complexity.

**Content creation**

| Action | Credits (approx) |
|---|---|
| Post creation | ~30 |
| Article creation | ~50 |
| Image generation | ~125 |
| Citations / fact-checking | ~30 |
| Hook & angle simulation | ~15 |
| Audio transcription | ~30 |
| Pin / save idea | ~15 |

**Interviews & brainstorm**

| Action | Credits (approx) |
|---|---|
| Interview or Brainstorm session | ~250 |
| Twin-guided interview | ~250 |
| Agent interview | ~70 |

**Content Twin**

| Action | Credits (approx) |
|---|---|
| Twin usage in content | ~100 |
| Twin training | ~250 per asset |
| Twin chat | ~100 |

**Agent Studio**

| Action | Credits (approx) |
|---|---|
| Agent chat | ~200 |
| Agent topic discovery | ~650 |
| Agent content calendar | ~500 |
| Trend analysis | ~600 |

Some features scale based on output depth. Agent Studio consumes more credits than single-step actions because it bundles ideation, agent reasoning, and content calendar planning across time horizons. Agent topic discovery and content calendars scale with research depth and planning scope. Twin training scales with the size of the training batch.

## Credits per plan

Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance:

| Plan | Monthly credits |
|---|---|
| Starter | 9,000 |
| Pro | 18,000 |
| Leader | 30,000 |
| Agency plans | Large shared pools |

## Rollover

Your plan's monthly credit allowance resets on your billing date each month. It's a quota, not a balance you accumulate, so any unused portion of that allowance does not carry over to the next month.

Additional credits you've purchased through a top-up or been granted are different: those carry over month to month and remain available until you use them.

## Top-ups

If you need more credits mid-cycle, you can purchase a top-up at any time. Top-ups are optional and are designed for heavy campaign periods, temporary spikes in activity, or larger agency and team workflows. You can also enable auto top-up to prevent interruptions, with a monthly cap to stay in control of spend.

<Note>
  You always see your remaining credits in the dashboard. Credits are deducted only for successful AI actions, errors, retries, and clarifications never consume credits.
</Note>
