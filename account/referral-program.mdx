Pressmaster has a referral program. You get a personal referral link. When someone signs up through it and later converts, the reward is recorded against your account.

## Who can use it

The referral program is available on Starter, Leader, and Partner plans. It isn't shown on the Free plan, and it isn't available on white-label or agency accounts.

## Find your referral link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your dashboard">
    Go to your Pressmaster dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Earn button">
    Click the **Earn $1,000** button in the lower-left corner of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the referral window">
    The referral window opens with your personal link and the available sharing options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or share">
    Copy your link, or share it using one of the options in the window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also ask your assistant: "What's my referral link?" It works in the web chat and in Telegram, and returns the same link you see in the referral window. If the program isn't available for your account, the assistant tells you so instead of giving a link.
</Tip>

## How sharing works

When you share through the referral window, the link leads to a landing page generated for you. The people you invite sign up through that page, and the signup is attributed to you. This works with email, Google, and Apple sign-up.

## Track your referrals

Open the referral window at any time to see:

- Your personal referral link
- How many people viewed your link
- Who signed up through it
- Your conversions and earned rewards

<Note>
  Rewards are processed once a day. New signups and rewards can take up to a day to appear in the window.
</Note>

The reward amount and the offer for the people you invite are shown in the referral window.

## Make sure a signup counts

- Share your referral link, not the Pressmaster homepage or your dashboard URL.
- Ask the person to open your link and sign up in the same browser, without switching devices in between.
- If you want to test the link yourself, use a private window and a new email address.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='The referral window shows "Referrals are temporarily unavailable"'>
    The message reads "Referrals are temporarily unavailable. Please try again later." It only affects the referral window. The rest of your account keeps working. Try again in a few minutes, and contact support if it persists.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see the Earn button">
    The program is available on Starter, Leader, and Partner plans only, and it's hidden on white-label and agency accounts. If you're on an eligible plan and still don't see it, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone signed up but doesn't appear in my window">
    Signups only count when the person signs up through your referral link. Check that they used the link, then contact support with their email address so the team can check the attribution.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My rewards show zero">
    Rewards are processed once a day and are recorded when a referral converts and meets the reward conditions. Check again the next day. If a referral has converted and the reward is still missing, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
