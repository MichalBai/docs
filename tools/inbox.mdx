Inbox is Pressmaster's in-app notification center, a single place to see everything that needs your attention across the platform, without hunting through each section individually.

## What shows up in Inbox

Inbox collects notifications across categories:

- **Comments**, new comments on your published posts
- **Approvals**, content and agent actions waiting for your review
- **Agent tasks**, updates on tasks your agents have run, completed, or flagged
- **Publishing**, confirmations and failures for scheduled and published content
- **Performance signals**, notable changes in your metrics worth knowing about
- **Account alerts**, billing, credits, and connection issues that need action

## Announcements

Announcements is a separate category inside Inbox for updates from the Pressmaster team, new features, changes to existing ones, and other platform news, kept apart from your workspace's own activity so the two don't get mixed together.

<Tip>
  Check Inbox regularly so approvals and account alerts don't sit unresolved, items like a disconnected channel or a pending approval can block other work until you act on them.
</Tip>
